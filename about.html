About Us - Punjabi Toffee Crush

Welcome to Punjabi Toffee Crush! This is a free educational game made for Punjabi kids living in India, Canada, USA, UK, Australia and all over the world. Our mission is very simple - to help kids learn their mother tongue Punjabi in a fun, safe and joyful way. Many Punjabi parents living abroad told us that their kids speak English at school, Hindi at home, but they slowly forget Punjabi Gurmukhi letters like Ura Aira Iri, Kakka Khakha Gagga, Punjabi numbers and simple words like Maa, Pani, Ghar. We decided to solve this problem with technology that kids love - games.

This project was started in 2026 by Deepali Tokhi, a mother, educator and Punjabi culture lover from Delhi, India. I saw in my own family that my kids were spending hours on violent games but not learning Punjabi. So I thought why not make a game that teaches Punjabi? A game where matching toffees improves memory, bursting bubbles with Ura Aira improves listening, and making words like Maa Pani Ghar teaches vocabulary. That idea became Punjabi Toffee Crush.

Our 6 Levels Explained

Level 1 Toffee Crush: Classic match 3 puzzle with toffees 🍪🍩🧁. Kids find 3 same toffees. This improves focus, memory and hand-eye coordination. No violence, only sweet fun.
Level 2 Ura Aira Bubble: Punjabi letters Ura ੳ, Aira ਅ, Iri ੲ fall from top. Kids listen to voice Uraa Airhaa Iree and burst correct bubble before it reaches bottom. Voice support in Hindi/Punjabi accent helps pronunciation.
Level 3 Kakka Khakha: Learn Ka Kha Ga ਘ. Same match 3 logic but with Gurmukhi letters. Foundation of Punjabi reading.
Level 4 Punjabi Numbers: Learn Gurmukhi numbers ੧ ੨ ੩ (Ek Do Teen). Important for counting in Punjabi.
Level 5 Shabad Making: Small daily Punjabi words Maa, Pani, Ghar. Kids learn meaning and spelling together.
Level 6 Final Boss: Mix of all 5 levels - ultimate challenge to become Punjabi Champion with crown 👑. Only kids who win first 5 can unlock this.

Why Parents and Teachers Trust Us?

No chat, no comments, no user generated content, no violent ads. We use only Google AdSense family-safe ads. Works on mobile, tablet, computer without install. No app needed. Teachers in Punjab schools use it in classrooms to teach Gurmukhi. Parents in Brampton, Surrey, Toronto, London Southall, Melbourne love it because kids finally start speaking Punjabi at home. This is not just a game, it is a movement to preserve Punjabi culture, language and heritage for next generation.

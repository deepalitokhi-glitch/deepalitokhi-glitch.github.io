1. How to Teach Punjabi to Kids Living in Canada, USA, UK?

Many parents in Brampton, Surrey, Toronto ask how to teach Punjabi when kids speak English at school. Best way is games, not books. Kids age 3-10 learn fast with visuals and sound. Start with Ura Aira Iri with voice. Play 10 minutes daily. Speak small Punjabi words at home like Pani de, Maa, Ghar chalo. Our Level 2 bubble game does exactly this - listen and burst correct letter. Don't force, make it fun. Reward with toffee when they win level.

2. Why Gurmukhi is Important?

Gurmukhi is not just letters, it is our culture, our Guru Granth Sahib language, our identity. If kids forget Gurmukhi, they lose connection to gurdwara, to grandparents stories. Learning Ka Kha Ga is first step to reading Japji Sahib later. Our game teaches Ka Kha Ga with match 3 logic so kids remember shape.

3. How Our 6 Level System Works for Memory?

Child psychologists say repetition with fun improves memory. Level 1 improves focus, Level 2 listening, Level 3 reading, Level 4 numbers, Level 5 vocabulary, Level 6 mixes all for final test. 9 points to win each level keeps motivation high. Lock system ensures kids don't skip. This is better than random YouTube videos.

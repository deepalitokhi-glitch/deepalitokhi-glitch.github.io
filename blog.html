2. Why Punjabi Language is Important for Next Generation?

Punjabi is not just language, it is culture. Guru Granth Sahib Ji is written in Gurmukhi. Our Bhangra, Giddha, folk songs are in Punjabi. If kids don't learn Punjabi, they will not understand our history.

Many schools in Punjab now teach English only. So kids need extra fun way to learn Punjabi. Games are best. This game is made by Deepali Tokhi in 2026 for all Punjabi kids worldwide - not only USA, but for India, Canada, USA, UK, Australia everyone.

About This Game

Punjabi Toffee Crush is a free educational game made by Deepali Tokhi from Delhi for kids age 3-10 years.

Kids can learn Punjabi Gurmukhi ੳ ਅ ੲ, Akhar ਕ ਖ ਗ, Numbers ੧ ੨ ੩ and simple words like ਮਾਂ, ਪਾਣੀ, ਘਰ in a fun way.

This game is 100% safe. No login, no personal data. Made with love for India, Canada, UK, USA, Australia.

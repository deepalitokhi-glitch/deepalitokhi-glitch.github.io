About Punjabi Learning for Kids

Punjabi is a beautiful language spoken by millions. Our game helps kids learn Gurmukhi Akhar (ੳ ਅ ੲ), Numbers (੧ ੨ ੩), and Words (ਮਾਂ, ਪਾਣੀ, ਘਰ) with fun Toffee theme. Each level is designed by Deepali Tokhi for easy learning.

How to Play?

Level 1: Match same 3 Toffees. Level 2: Tap falling Bindi. Level 3-6: Match Akhar, Numbers, Words. Win all 6 levels to become Champion!

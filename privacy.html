Privacy Policy - Punjabi Toffee Crush

Last Updated: October 13, 2026. At Punjabi Toffee Crush, we take your privacy and especially children's privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how our educational game website works, what information we do and do not collect, and how we keep kids safe online. This policy applies to https://deepalitokhi-glitch.github.io/ and all its pages about.html, privacy.html, contact.html, blog.html.

1. No Personal Data Collection

We do NOT collect any personal information. No name, no email, no phone number, no address, no login, no password, no date of birth. You can play game without giving any information. This is by design to keep kids safe.

2. LocalStorage Only

We use browser localStorage to remember your unlocked level. Key name is punjabi_level. It stores only a number 1 to 6. This data stays in your device only. We cannot access it, we do not send it to any server. If you click Reset Game, it becomes 1 again.

3. Children's Privacy COPPA Compliant

This game is made for kids 3 to 10 years. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. There is no chat, no comment, no upload, no user generated content, no contact with strangers. Game is single player offline logic.

These cookies are managed by Google, not by us. You can opt out of personalized ads by visiting https://adssettings.google.com. We have blocked sensitive categories like adult, gambling, alcohol, violent content. All ads are family safe. We do not have any other third party trackers.

5. No Data Sharing or Selling

Since we collect no data, we share no data and sell no data. Simple.

6. External Links

Our footer has only internal links to our own pages. We have no external links except Google AdSense which is ad network.

7. Security

Website is hosted on GitHub Pages with HTTPS encryption. No data is transmitted to our own server because we have no server. Game runs fully in your browser.

If we update this policy, we will update date at top. For any privacy question, contact us via contact.html page. By using our site, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use site. Thank you for trusting Punjabi Toffee Crush to keep your kids safe while learning Punjabi.
